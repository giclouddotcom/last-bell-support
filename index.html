Last Bell

Privacy policy and support · Effective 24 November 2026

Support

Questions, bug reports or trouble joining a friend's game: email support@apexgtm.ai. Tell us your iPhone model and what happened.

The short version

Last Bell does not ask for, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.

Information on your device

Your player name, chosen student, cleared chapters and settings are saved only on your device. Like other app data, they are included in your device backups (iCloud or your computer), which Apple or you control. Deleting the app deletes them.

Playing with friends

Solo play and the tutorial run entirely on your device. When you create or join a co-op game, the app connects to the Last Bell game server, hosted on Amazon Web Services in Singapore. The server receives the name you type (a nickname is fine), your moves and the game's passcode, and shares your name and moves only with the other players in that game.

The server keeps a game in its memory only while it is being played. A game is deleted 2 minutes after its last player leaves, and every game ends when the server restarts. Names and moves are never written to disk, and the server's logs record only how many games and players are running, and errors.

To stop people guessing passcodes or flooding the server, it counts the new games and wrong passcodes from each internet address for 10 minutes, in memory only, and then forgets them.

Children

Last Bell is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
